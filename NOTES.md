# Notes — Five Seconds to Explain the Glass

Final sentence (53 characters, 1 sentence, no trailing newline):

"The glass insulted my tail, so I showed it the floor."

Submit title: The Glass Insulted My Tail

Why this wins: the cat never apologises and never confesses — it
counter-accuses. The glass started it by insulting the tail, so knocking
it down is presented as a proportionate, almost polite response, which is
pure cat logic: self-interest, zero remorse, and the blame neatly flipped
onto the victim. The idea is fresh — not gravity, not a slippery edge, not
"it was already falling" — and the second beat lands on a concrete image
you can see (the glass on the floor) instead of an abstract concept.
Spoken in one breath, it fits the five seconds.
